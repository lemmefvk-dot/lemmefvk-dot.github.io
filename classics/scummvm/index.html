StaticOS Free Classics: Your own ScummVM games

The ScummVM Team · your own copy of the game. Licence and source for the copy on StaticOS’s Free Classics shelf.

ScummVM is free software by The ScummVM Team that plays many old adventure games from their original files. On StaticOS it plays your own copy of a game: you pick its folder on your device, and its files are read in your browser and handed only to the engine in its sealed frame. They are never uploaded, and they are kept on your device only if you tick “Keep”, for you alone. StaticOS supplies no games here, and the ScummVM Team has nothing to do with StaticOS.

This build has these engines: SCUMM (with v7 and v8, and Humongous), AGI, SCI (with SCI32), AGOS (with AGOS 2), Kyra (the Kyrandia games), Broken Sword and Broken Sword II, Flight of the Amazon Queen, Beneath a Steel Sky, Lure of the Temptress, Drascula, SAGA (with I Have No Mouth), Tinsel, Gobli*ns, Cinématique (evo 1 and 2), MADE, Parallaction, Touche, Bud Tucker, Dreamweb and Toonstruck. CD music is played when your copy has it as Ogg Vorbis files; MP3 and FLAC are not built in, and neither are MIDI synthesisers beyond the built-in AdLib emulation.
