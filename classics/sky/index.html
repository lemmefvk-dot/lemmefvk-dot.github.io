StaticOS Free Classics: Beneath a Steel Sky

Revolution Software, 1994. Licence and source for the copy on StaticOS’s Free Classics shelf.

Beneath a Steel Sky was made by Revolution Software Ltd in 1994, with art by Dave Gibbons. Revolution released this version as freeware. It runs here on ScummVM, by The ScummVM Team. StaticOS did not make this game and does not own it, and neither Revolution nor the ScummVM Team has anything to do with StaticOS.
