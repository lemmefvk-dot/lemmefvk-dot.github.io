StaticOS Free Classics: Sopwith

David L. Clark, BMB Compuscience · SDL port by Simon Howard, 1984. Licence and source for the copy on StaticOS’s Free Classics shelf.

Sopwith was written by David L. Clark at BMB Compuscience Canada in 1984. He later released its source under the GNU GPL. This is SDL Sopwith, a port by Simon Howard and contributors, slightly modified to run inside StaticOS. StaticOS did not make this game.
